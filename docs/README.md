# Softonix AI Photo & Video Studio

Softonix AI Studio is a full-stack, production-grade SaaS media enhancement platform. It enables users to upload bursts of 5-20 photos or short video clips to automatically extract, align, enhance, and upscale images into high-definition outputs.

## Highlights & Features
- **Smart Frame Selection:** Automated video frame extraction via OpenCV.
- **Laplacian Quality Scoring:** Drops blurry or duplicate frames automatically.
- **Face Detection & Alignment:** Levels and centers eyes and faces.
- **Multi-Provider AI Architecture:** Plug-and-play provider layer for Mock, Replicate, Fal AI, or Hugging Face.
- **Modern Glassmorphism UI:** Built with pure Vanilla HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript.
